# END PUTIN
## A Declaration of Crimes Against Humanity, a Demand for Justice, and an Ultimatum for Unconditional Surrender

**November 6, 2026**

### To the Peoples and Governments of the World

When, in the course of human events, a ruler has abandoned the obligations of government, subjected his own people to oppression, invaded sovereign nations, and carried violence beyond his borders, a decent respect for the opinions of mankind requires that the causes of resistance be declared before the world.

We hold these truths to be self-evident: that all human beings are equal in dignity and rights; that governments derive their legitimate authority from the consent of the governed; and that no ruler possesses the right to extinguish the liberty of his own people or the sovereignty of another.

That life, liberty, and the pursuit of happiness are not privileges bestowed by governments, but rights that governments are established to protect.

That the deliberate murder of political opponents, the persecution of dissent, and the conquest of neighboring nations are incompatible with these principles.

And that when a government abandons these obligations, the international community has a responsibility to uphold the law, defend the innocent, and pursue accountability.

The history of Vladimir Vladimirovich Putin's rule is a history of repeated injuries against these principles, committed first against the people of Russia, then against Ukraine, and increasingly against the peoples of Europe.

**To prove this, let facts be submitted to a candid world.**

## I. His Crimes Against the Russian People

**He has** dismantled the institutions of Russian democracy, consolidated political authority, suppressed independent journalism, and deprived the Russian people of meaningful political competition.

**He has** employed the instruments of government to persecute political opponents, intimidate independent businesses, imprison journalists, and silence citizens who oppose his policies.

**He has** presided over a system in which critics of the government have been murdered, poisoned, imprisoned, driven into exile, or subjected to political prosecution.

Among the most consequential cases are those of Boris Nemtsov, Vladimir Kara-Murza, Sergei Magnitsky, and Alexei Navalny.

### Boris Nemtsov — The Assassination of Political Opposition

**He stands accused** of ordering the assassination of Boris Nemtsov, a former deputy prime minister and one of Russia's most prominent democratic opposition leaders.

On February 27, 2015, Nemtsov was shot dead on a bridge overlooking the Kremlin.

He had publicly opposed Russia's aggression against Ukraine and challenged the government's conduct.

Five men were convicted for participating in his murder.

Yet the European Court of Human Rights concluded in July 2023 that Russian authorities had failed to adequately investigate who commissioned and organized the assassination, including credible allegations concerning political motives and possible involvement of state officials.

A government capable of investigating its enemies demonstrated no comparable determination to expose the sponsors of this political murder.

**The murder of Boris Nemtsov remains an indictment of the corrupt justice system of the Russian state.**

### Vladimir Kara-Murza — The Persecution of the Living

**He has** subjected Vladimir Kara-Murza, a Russian opposition leader and advocate of international human rights sanctions, to political imprisonment and persecution.

Kara-Murza survived two suspected poisoning attacks, in 2015 and 2017, which nearly killed him.

He continued to advocate democratic government, accountability for political assassinations, and sanctions against perpetrators of human rights abuses.

In April 2023, a Russian court sentenced him to 25 years in prison following his condemnation of the invasion of Ukraine and his political activities.

He endured harsh imprisonment before being released in an international prisoner exchange on August 1, 2024.

The Parliamentary Assembly of the Council of Europe called for targeted sanctions against the officials responsible for his persecution and ill-treatment.

His release does not erase his persecution. Nor does it resolve the suspected attempts upon his life.

Kara-Murza's survival stands as evidence that Russian political resistance remains alive despite the state's efforts to suppress it.

### Sergei Magnitsky — The Murderous Consequences of Corruption

**He has** presided over a system in which exposing official corruption can cost a citizen his freedom and his life.

Sergei Magnitsky, a Russian tax and legal adviser investigating a $230 million tax-refund fraud involving stolen corporate identities and corrupt Russian officials, was arrested in November 2008.

He spent 358 days in detention.

He was subjected to abusive conditions, denied adequate medical treatment, and died on November 16, 2009, aged 37.

In 2019, the European Court of Human Rights established that Russian authorities had violated his right to life, subjected him to ill-treatment, failed to investigate his death adequately, and violated his fair-trial rights.

The Russian state subsequently prosecuted and convicted Magnitsky after his death.

His name now identifies an international framework for sanctions against human rights violators and corrupt officials.

**The Russian government sought to prosecute Magnitsky even in death. His name instead became an instrument of accountability around the world.**

### Alexei Navalny — Poisoning, Imprisonment, and Death

**He stands accused** of assassinating Alexei Navalny, the opposition leader who exposed corruption among Russia's political elite.

Navalny survived a Novichok nerve-agent poisoning in 2020.

He returned to Russia, was imprisoned, and died in an Arctic penal colony on February 16, 2024.

In February 2026, the governments of the United Kingdom, Sweden, France, Germany, and the Netherlands announced that laboratory analysis had established the presence of the lethal toxin epibatidine in samples from Navalny's body.

Those governments concluded that poisoning was highly likely to have caused his death.

Russia has disputed allegations of state responsibility.

Navalny's death demands full judicial accountability, including investigation of those who ordered, facilitated, or concealed any criminal acts.

## II. The Accounting of Political Assassinations

These four men do not stand alone.

The Putin era has witnessed the murders of politicians, journalists, human rights defenders, business figures, and critics of the Russian state.

Their names shall not be lost to history.

Among those murdered are:

- **Igor Domnikov (2000)** — Investigative journalist, fatally beaten in Moscow.
- **Sergei Novikov (2000)** — Independent broadcaster, murdered in Smolensk.
- **Vladimir Golovlyov (2002)** — Liberal Russia parliamentarian, assassinated in Moscow.
- **Valentin Tsvetkov (2002)** — Governor of Magadan Oblast, assassinated in Moscow.
- **Sergei Yushenkov (2003)** — Liberal Russia opposition leader and investigator of the 1999 apartment bombings, shot dead outside his home.
- **Nikolai Girenko (2004)** — Human rights defender and anti-racism researcher, murdered by extremists.
- **Paul Klebnikov (2004)** — Editor of *Forbes Russia*, assassinated after investigating corruption and organized crime.
- **Zelimkhan Yandarbiyev (2004)** — Former Chechen separatist leader, killed in Qatar in a bombing attributed by a Qatari court to Russian intelligence officers.
- **Andrei Kozlov (2006)** — Senior Russian central banker involved in combating financial misconduct, assassinated in Moscow.
- **Anna Politkovskaya (2006)** — Investigative journalist documenting abuses in Chechnya, murdered in her apartment building. Those who commissioned the assassination have not been conclusively identified.
- **Alexander Litvinenko (2006)** — Former Russian security officer poisoned with polonium-210 in London. A British public inquiry concluded that the operation was probably approved by Vladimir Putin.
- **Magomed Yevloyev (2008)** — Opposition journalist and publisher, fatally shot while in police custody.
- **Ruslan Yamadayev (2008)** — Former parliamentarian and rival of Chechen political authorities, assassinated in Moscow.
- **Stanislav Markelov (2009)** — Human rights lawyer murdered in Moscow.
- **Anastasia Baburova (2009)** — Journalist murdered alongside Markelov.
- **Umar Israilov (2009)** — Chechen exile who alleged torture by Chechen authorities, assassinated in Vienna.
- **Natalya Estemirova (2009)** — Human rights investigator exposing abuses in Chechnya, abducted and murdered.
- **Gadzhimurad Kamalov (2011)** — Founder of the independent Dagestani newspaper *Chernovik*, assassinated.
- **Akhmednabi Akhmednabiyev (2013)** — Investigative journalist murdered in Dagestan.
- **Boris Nemtsov (2015)** — Opposition leader assassinated near the Kremlin.
- **Zelimkhan Khangoshvili (2019)** — Georgian citizen of Chechen origin, murdered in Berlin. A German court established that the killing was commissioned by Russian state authorities.

Other deaths and attempted killings require continued investigation, including those involving **Yuri Shchekochikhin, Timur Kuashev, Mikhail Beketov, Vladimir Kara-Murza, Alexei Navalny, and Sergei Skripal**.

These cases do not all have the same evidentiary status. Some are judicially established state operations; others are documented murders with unresolved sponsors, suspected poisonings, or deaths under disputed circumstances.

We do not declare Putin personally responsible for every killing without proof.

We declare that his government must answer for its own conduct, that the perpetrators and organizers must be investigated, and that political violence must never receive the protection of official impunity.

## III. His Persecution of Business, Property, and Independent Enterprise

**He has** weaponized taxation, prosecution, regulatory power, and state security institutions against businesspeople who exposed corruption or challenged political authority.

The persecution of **Sir William Browder and Hermitage Capital Management** provides a documented example.

Browder, once among the largest foreign portfolio investors in Russia, was barred from entering the country in 2005.

In 2007, the corporate identities of Hermitage-associated companies were stolen and used in a fraudulent $230 million tax-refund scheme involving corrupt officials.

Sergei Magnitsky helped expose that fraud.

Following Magnitsky's death, Browder campaigned for sanctions and international accountability.

Russian authorities pursued Browder through criminal proceedings and attempts to obtain his arrest internationally.

In 2013, INTERPOL publicly concluded that Russia's attempt to use its channels against Browder was predominantly political and contrary to its rules.

Browder has testified before Western legislatures about the continuing campaign of legal harassment, threats, and efforts to discredit him.

Nor was this the only confrontation between the Russian state and independent business.

**Mikhail Khodorkovsky and Yukos** became the subjects of criminal prosecution and the dismantling of one of Russia's largest private enterprises.

International arbitration proceedings resulted in findings of unlawful expropriation and awards exceeding $50 billion to former Yukos shareholders, although Russia has continued to contest the awards.

**He has** fostered a system in which private property and commercial independence remain vulnerable to political power.

A nation cannot sustain economic liberty when the state may punish independent enterprise for exposing corruption or challenging authority.

## IV. His Undeclared War Against Europe

**He has** extended Russian hostile operations beyond Ukraine through covert and deniable activities directed against European nations.

These activities, commonly described as *hybrid warfare*, include sabotage, cyberattacks, information manipulation, interference with critical infrastructure, airspace violations, and attempted assassinations.

Their purpose, as assessed by European institutions, is to destabilize democracies, weaken support for Ukraine, and test the defenses of neighboring states while remaining below the threshold of conventional war.

**He has** presided over intelligence operations linked to incendiary parcels transported through European logistics networks.

In March 2026, Eurojust announced that investigators from several European countries had identified 22 suspects believed to have participated in operations on behalf of Russian military intelligence.

Incendiary parcels had ignited in Germany, Poland, and the United Kingdom, endangering transport infrastructure and creating risks to civilian life.

**He has** presided over a campaign of covert interference involving drones, unidentified aerial incursions, cyber operations, and attacks or suspected attacks on infrastructure.

A 2026 investigation coordinated by the European Broadcasting Union documented hundreds of reported physical hybrid incidents and plots across 17 European countries between April and October 2026. The strength of attribution to Russia varied between incidents.

The International Institute for Strategic Studies separately assessed that a Russian-linked drone campaign had likely operated across European airspace.

These are the instruments of a shadow war: operations designed to intimidate, disrupt, and evade open responsibility.

Not every unidentified drone, infrastructure failure, or suspicious incident is proven to be a Russian attack.

Yet the European Union has formally condemned Russia's sustained hybrid campaigns and imposed sanctions in response.

**The sovereignty of European nations does not cease to exist merely because an attack is concealed.**

## V. His Wars in Chechnya and Georgia, and the Destruction of Civilian Aircraft

### Chechnya — The Destruction of Grozny

**He has** presided over Russian military campaigns in Chechnya involving indiscriminate bombardment, extrajudicial executions, torture, enforced disappearances, and the devastation of civilian communities.

Beginning in 1999, during Putin's tenure as prime minister and subsequently president, Russian forces conducted operations that brought widespread destruction to Grozny and surrounding settlements.

Human Rights Watch documented massacres at Alkhan-Yurt, Staropromyslovski, and Novye Aldi, alongside systematic abuses against civilians.

The European Court of Human Rights subsequently established Russian responsibility for unlawful killings, disproportionate military attacks, and failures to investigate abuses in numerous cases arising from the conflict.

These included attacks on civilians fleeing Grozny and the bombing of Katyr-Yurt in February 2000.

The abuses continued under the system of government established in Chechnya with Moscow's support.

**The destruction of Chechnya stands among the earliest and most extensively documented human rights catastrophes of Putin's rule.**

### Georgia — The Invasion and Occupation of Sovereign Territory

**He has** participated in the leadership of a Russian state that used military force against Georgia in August 2008 and subsequently entrenched Russian control over Abkhazia and South Ossetia.

The war resulted in civilian deaths, destruction, displacement, and the separation of families from their homes and communities.

The European Court of Human Rights established that Russia exercised effective control over South Ossetia, Abkhazia, and other areas following the active hostilities, and found Russia responsible for serious violations, including unlawful killings, torture, destruction of property, and interference with civilian movement.

In 2024, the Court further identified a pattern of abuses arising from the continued division of Georgian territory.

In October 2025, it awarded more than €253 million in compensation concerning over 29,000 victims of those violations.

**The sovereignty of Georgia has never been extinguished by the presence of Russian forces.**

### Malaysia Airlines Flight MH17 — 298 Lives Extinguished

**He has** presided over a military and intelligence apparatus whose actions contributed to the destruction of a civilian passenger aircraft and the deaths of every person aboard.

On July 17, 2014, Malaysia Airlines Flight MH17 was shot down over eastern Ukraine by a Russian-made Buk surface-to-air missile.

All **298 passengers and crew** were killed.

The international Joint Investigation Team established that the missile system originated from Russia's 53rd Anti-Aircraft Missile Brigade.

In November 2022, a Dutch court convicted three men for their involvement in the destruction of the aircraft.

On May 12, 2025, the Council of the International Civil Aviation Organization concluded that the Russian Federation was responsible for violating international civil aviation law in the downing of MH17.

On July 9, 2025, the European Court of Human Rights separately found Russia responsible for the destruction of the aircraft and the deaths of those aboard.

**These findings establish Russian state responsibility. They do not, by themselves, establish that Putin personally ordered the attack.**

The families of the victims are entitled to accountability, full disclosure, and reparations.

### Azerbaijan Airlines Flight 8243 — Thirty-Eight More Dead

**He has** presided over Russian air-defense operations that caused the destruction of another civilian aircraft.

On December 25, 2024, Azerbaijan Airlines Flight J2-8243 sustained damage while approaching Grozny and subsequently crashed near Aktau, Kazakhstan.

**Thirty-eight people were killed.**

On October 9, 2025, Vladimir Putin acknowledged that Russian air defenses had been responsible for the incident.

He attributed the disaster to Russian missiles fired toward Ukrainian drones and said compensation would follow.

The acknowledged Russian military involvement does not establish that the passenger aircraft was deliberately targeted.

But the absence of an established intent to shoot down civilians does not absolve the responsible authorities of the obligation to investigate the deaths, establish negligence or other culpability where supported by evidence, and provide reparations.

**The skies must not become another theater in which civilians bear the fatal consequences of Russian military operations.**

## VI. His War of Aggression Against Ukraine

**He has** violated the sovereignty and territorial integrity of Ukraine through military aggression, beginning with the occupation and attempted annexation of Crimea in 2014 and escalating into the full-scale invasion of February 24, 2022.

**He has** presided over military operations resulting in the deaths and injuries of thousands of Ukrainian civilians and the destruction of homes, schools, hospitals, and essential infrastructure.

**He has** presided over the unlawful deportation and forcible transfer of Ukrainian children.

On March 17, 2023, the International Criminal Court issued a warrant for Vladimir Putin's arrest, finding reasonable grounds to believe he bears individual criminal responsibility for alleged war crimes involving the unlawful deportation and transfer of children.

In March 2026, the United Nations Independent International Commission of Inquiry on Ukraine concluded that Russian authorities had committed crimes against humanity through the deportation, forcible transfer, and enforced disappearance of Ukrainian children.

**He has** presided over Russian military operations involving torture, sexual violence, enforced disappearances, unlawful detention, and abuses of prisoners of war documented by international investigators.

**He has** subjected Ukrainian civilians to repeated attacks on energy infrastructure, depriving communities of electricity, heating, and essential services during winter.

In October 2026, the United Nations Commission of Inquiry concluded that Russian authorities had committed war crimes and crimes against humanity through their sustained attacks on Ukraine's energy infrastructure.

**He has** maintained the invasion in defiance of repeated international demands for the withdrawal of Russian forces.

The invasion is not a legitimate means of acquiring territory.

The sovereignty of Ukraine is not subject to the ambitions of Vladimir Putin.

## VII. His Destruction of Russia's Future Generations

**He has** inflicted an irreversible loss of Russian lives and aggravated a demographic crisis whose consequences will burden his nation for generations.

According to the Center for Strategic and International Studies, Russian forces suffered approximately **1.4 million battlefield casualties, including an estimated 400,000 to 450,000 deaths**, between February 2022 and June 2026.

These are estimates, not independently verified totals.

He has directed a war that has taken hundreds of thousands of Russian lives and driven many other citizens, including highly educated professionals, to seek their futures abroad.

The Russian Federation entered this war with serious demographic problems already established.

Those problems were intensified by military mortality, displacement, uncertainty, and the disruption of families and economic life.

The United Nations' 2024 medium-variant projection anticipates a reduction of approximately **10 million people in Russia's population between 2024 and 2054**.

That projected decline is not inevitable, nor can it all be attributed to the invasion. Russia's low fertility and population aging long predate the war.

But the deaths already suffered cannot be reversed.

A generation of productive lives has been lost to combat, imprisonment, displacement, and state repression.

**He has squandered the human capital of Russia and imposed consequences that will endure long after his government has passed into history.**

The children who will never know their fathers, the families permanently divided, the lives never lived, and the opportunities destroyed must also be entered into the accounting of this war.

## VIII. Our Appeal to the Russian People

We distinguish the Russian people from the government that rules them.

We recognize their equal right to life, liberty, security, and democratic self-government.

We recognize the courage of Russians who have resisted political oppression, opposed the invasion of Ukraine, exposed official corruption, and demanded justice for the murdered and imprisoned.

Our demands are not for the destruction of Russia.

Nor do we seek the collective punishment of its citizens.

We call upon Russian soldiers to refuse unlawful orders, protect civilians, respect the rights of prisoners, and lay down their arms rather than perpetuate aggression.

We call upon Russian authorities to cease political persecution and cooperate with international criminal investigations.

We call upon the Russian people to determine their own future through the peaceful exercise of their fundamental rights.

## IX. Our Demands

We therefore declare that the following conditions are necessary for an enduring and just peace:

1. **Full and unconditional cessation of Russia's war of aggression against Ukraine**, including the withdrawal of Russian armed forces from all internationally recognized Ukrainian territory, including Crimea.

2. **The surrender of Vladimir Putin to competent international judicial authority** to answer the outstanding arrest warrant and other charges supported by evidence, with all guarantees of due process.

3. **The immediate return of every unlawfully deported or transferred Ukrainian child** and the release of civilians unlawfully detained.

4. **Independent investigation and accountability for political assassinations, poisonings, torture, unlawful detention, atrocities in Chechnya, and other serious human rights abuses**, including crimes committed within Russia and abroad.

5. **Full cooperation with investigations into military aggression against Georgia, the destruction of civilian aircraft, and covert attacks against European nations**, including sabotage, assassinations, and attacks on civilian infrastructure.

6. **Restitution, reparations, and enforceable safeguards** against renewed aggression, accompanied by legal accountability for corruption, unlawful expropriation, and politically motivated abuse of judicial authority.

7. **The protection of Russia's future democratic self-government**, free from foreign-imposed rule, collective punishment, or reprisals against civilians.

## X. Our Final Declaration

We appeal to the governments and peoples of the world to uphold international law and Ukraine's right of self-defense.

**We demand that Vladimir Putin surrender fully and unconditionally to lawful justice and bring his war of aggression to an end.**

If he refuses, we call upon nations acting lawfully in collective defense with Ukraine to provide the protection necessary to secure its skies.

To intercept the missiles and drones directed against its people.

To protect its cities, its energy infrastructure, its hospitals, and its homes.

And to defeat and disable the Russian military forces conducting the invasion, ending their capacity to wage further aggression against Ukraine through lawful military means.

Such action must respect international humanitarian law, including the principles of distinction, proportionality, and precaution.

Our objective is the defense of Ukraine and the termination of aggression, not the conquest of Russia.

Let no government claim ignorance of the evidence.

The political murders have been documented.

The corruption has been exposed.

The atrocities in Chechnya have been investigated.

The sovereignty of Georgia has been violated.

The passengers aboard civilian aircraft have been killed.

The victims of torture have testified.

The deported children have been identified.

The civilian graves have been counted.

The destruction of Ukraine has been witnessed by the world.

And the Russian people themselves have paid an enormous price.

**We therefore solemnly declare that Ukraine is, and of right ought to remain, a free, sovereign, and independent nation.**

Its territory is not Vladimir Putin's to claim.

Its people are not his to govern.

Its children are not his to take.

Its future is not his to decide.

Nor are the people of Russia his property to sacrifice in the pursuit of imperial ambition.

For the preservation of these rights, the defense of sovereign nations, and the pursuit of justice for the victims of aggression and political repression, we affirm our commitment to the independence of Ukraine and to the equal dignity of all peoples.

**The war of aggression must end. Its perpetrators must answer before the law. Ukraine must remain free.**

**Слава Україні. Героям слава.**
